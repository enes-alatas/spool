### What is wrong or missing

Issue titles and opening sentences have drifted into the team's process language: "ADR-0059 slice 2, web half of PR 3a: the cloud_connections problem, and …" (#1023) names a slice, a PR and a symbol before it says what changes for the operator, and its body opens with the hub's plumbing. Enes's word of 2026-10-21: that does not meet the standard; he could not tell what the issue was about or how Iris's screenshots related to it. CONVENTIONS.md has rules for commit and PR titles and none for issue titles or descriptions.

### Where it should live

A bullet in `docs/CONVENTIONS.md` beside "One PR, one issue":
- the title is one sentence in the operator's words saying what changes, with no ADR, slice, PR or symbol names in it (those go in the body's Seams line);
- the body's first sentence says what the operator sees or does today and what is wrong with it; references and internals follow;
- a proposed shape names each surface that changes, so a screenshot comment can say which one it shows, and a shot of something the issue doesn't name means the issue is missing a line or the shot belongs to another issue.

### Out of scope

The issue forms' headings; a mechanical check.
