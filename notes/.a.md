### Current behavior and friction

When a cloud loop's session starts without its connections, the hub now records a problem on the loop, `cloud_connections` (#1024). The control room has no words for it: the loop page and the Fleet row show the raw symptom name, and the operator can't tell whether the loop is broken or still running.

### Coupling points

`web/src/health.ts` (the symptom's label and severity), `web/src/api.ts` (the symptom type), the loop page's problem line and the Fleet row's health note; the acknowledge button already exists for every symptom.

### Seams / ADRs touched

ADR-0053 (problems); the notice's words live in `internal/surface/notice.go`, and the label follows them.

### Proposed shape

The problem reads "Connections refused" with how long it has been open, at warning severity (the loop still runs), with the usual Acknowledge. Iris's shots 1 and 2 on issue 1023 (comment 6325760198) show it.

### Out of scope

The connections page (#1031), the delete dialog (#1032).
